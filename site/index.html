3D sculpting

OxyBrush

Sculpt 3D models like digital clay: subdivision levels, dynamic detail, retopology, vertex color and posing, with glTF, OBJ, STL and PLY export.

3D characters

OxyCharacter

Parametric character creator: shape a base human with sliders, refit its skeleton automatically, dress it, and export a rigged glTF.

GitHub

OxyConvert

Desktop studio and command-line tool that turns scripts, macros and plugins from other sculpting tools into OxyBrush plugins.

About

Small studio, open work

Oxytocin Labs is an independent studio making tools for people who create: sculptors, artists, writers and the communities around them. Every app is built in public, shaped by its testers, and released early and often.

  • Made in the open. Code, issues and releases all live on GitHub.
  • Native and fast. Most apps are written in Rust and run on your own machine.
  • Shaped by testers. Feedback comes in through GitHub issues and Discord.
